Tabs, without AjaxTabbedPanel

A working tabbed panel built from two primitives: AjaxUpdateContainer (the content region) and AjaxUpdateLink (the tab clicks). The selected tab is just a field on the component; a click sets it and updates the container, which re-renders the strip (highlighting the active tab) and the matching panel. No JavaScript, no child components, no AjaxTabbedPanel.

Overview

The big picture. This panel shows because selectedTab equals "Overview".

Details

The fine print. Each panel is just a WOKeyValueConditional keyed on the same field the tabs set.

Settings

Knobs and dials. Add a tab by adding a name to the list and a panel here - nothing else.
