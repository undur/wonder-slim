AjaxBrowser (multi-select)

A <wo:AjaxBrowser multiple="$true"> - the multi-select form of wonder-select. Pick several fruits (the dropdown stays open, selections show as removable tags). The same widget as AjaxPopUpButton, driven by the select's multiple attribute. Inside a morphing container to confirm multi survives a morph.
